JOE MASTER UNIVERSE - music folders

Joe's theme is in music/background. With no intro track, the theme restarts and turns up for each entrance.

  music/background   Played on a loop (shuffled) in the lobby and during matches.
  music/intro        Joe Master's entrance music. Plays during every match intro and when someone wins.
                     If there are several files, one is picked at random each time.

Supported: .mp3 .m4a .ogg .wav .flac .aac .opus .webm
On GitHub, the music list updates automatically after you upload (see README). With the local server, no restart is needed.
In the game, press N to turn music on/off.
